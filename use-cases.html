Five rule packs on one engine

The pipeline is the same every time: read, map to a dated framework version, cite the passage, record the trail, route to a named reviewer. Only the rulebook changes.

Where this stands today. Rule pack 01 is available and runs end to end. Pack 02 is in development; packs 03 to 05 are planned — described here as designed, not delivered.
RULE PACK 01 Available

Dual-use and biosecurity screening

Screens research proposals for content that could create biosecurity or dual-use risk, before funding is committed.

What it does

  • Reads proposals, protocols and annexes in full — not titles and abstracts, where the risk rarely sits
  • Assesses against US DURC and pandemic-potential criteria, EU dual-use regulation and Australia Group lists
  • Flags the criterion touched and links to the passage that triggered it
  • Routes low-confidence items to human review, and drafts the reviewer's memo

Outcome

Coverage moves from a shortlist to the full portfolio, every decision carrying its own evidence.

Buyer

Head of research security, programme director — at funders, product development partnerships and foundations.

RULE PACK 02 In development

Export control and technology transfer

Designed to screen technical content and intangible transfer against export control lists, recording which dated list version applied.

What it is designed to do

  • Reads collaboration agreements, manuscripts, technical exchanges and transfer packages
  • Assesses against EU 2021/821 Annex I and the underlying regime lists
  • Records the list version in force at screening, so the decision can be defended later
  • Flags intangible transfer — the least-tooled, highest-volume part of the regulation

Intended outcome

Compliance can screen technical content, not only catalogued goods — and prove what against.

Buyer

Head of trade compliance, export control manager — pharma, biotech, CDMOs. The budget line exists.

RULE PACK 03 Planned

Pharmacovigilance case triage

Designed to triage adverse event cases and draft the narrative, inside a validated environment that writes back under change control.

What it is designed to do

  • Ingests case intake from the safety database and source documents
  • Assesses seriousness and expectedness against the reference safety information
  • Suggests coding and detects duplicates, with the evidence for each
  • Drafts the narrative for review, and writes back with e-signature and audit trail

Intended outcome

Handling time per case falls against a number the organisation already measures and reports.

Buyer

Head of pharmacovigilance, drug safety operations — mid-size sponsors and CROs.

RULE PACK 04 Planned

Deviation and CAPA investigation

Designed to assemble the evidence base for a quality investigation, on top of the existing quality system rather than in place of it.

What it is designed to do

  • Pulls batch records, prior deviations, related CAPAs and relevant SOP sections
  • Surfaces precedent — comparable events, what was concluded, what was implemented
  • Proposes root causes with the evidence for and against each
  • Drafts the rationale in your own format, and writes back under change control

Intended outcome

Investigations close faster, and the reasoning remains reconstructable at inspection years later.

Buyer

QA director, head of quality — manufacturers and CDMOs.

RULE PACK 05 Planned

Regulatory submission consistency

Designed to check a submission for internal contradiction across modules before it goes to the agency.

What it is designed to do

  • Reads across submission modules and supporting documents as one corpus
  • Detects contradictions in figures, claims, dates and cross-references
  • Traces every finding to section and document version
  • Re-runs against a revision and reports what changed

Intended outcome

Avoidable deficiency letters are caught internally, where they cost days rather than months.

Buyer

Head of regulatory operations — sponsors and regulatory affairs.
