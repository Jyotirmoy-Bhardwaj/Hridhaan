About

We build the evidence, not the opinion

Hridhaan Systems builds document assessment for regulated decisions: read the documents, map them against a dated version of the rulebook that governs them, produce findings that cite their own evidence. The judgement stays with the qualified human. What we remove is the reading, and the week of assembling proof afterwards.

What we think is true

Intelligence gets cheaper. Accountability does not.

01

The reading problem is solved

Frontier models read technical documents well, and get better every release. Building a business on reading them slightly better is building on someone else's roadmap.

02

The evidence problem is not

A regulated decision has to be reproducible, traceable to a dated rulebook, and defensible years later to someone who was not there. No general assistant offers that, and offering it works against their business model.

03

Curation is labour, not intelligence

A model knows the world. It does not know your organisation's reading of Annex I, or your SOP hierarchy. Building that — with your experts, against your historic decisions — is work that model releases do not erode.

04

Someone has to sign

Validation for intended use requires a named accountable party. The foundation model vendors will not be that party. Taking on that accountability, contractually, is the product.

How we work

Fixed scope, your documents, written success criteria

We do not start with a licence negotiation or a twelve-month programme. We start with four weeks of work on your own documents, against your own frameworks, inside your own environment.

Before

Criteria agreed in writing

What good looks like, which criteria are critical, and what would constitute failure — settled before any work starts, not argued about afterwards.

During

A measured baseline

How long the task takes you today, and how consistently your own reviewers agree with each other. Most organisations have never measured this.

After

A working prototype, and a decision

Something that runs on your documents, plus an honest route to production — or a clear reason not to proceed. Both are acceptable outcomes.
